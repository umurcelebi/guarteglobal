ABOUT GUARTE

More Than Trade
A Stronger Tomorrow

Headquartered in Thermi, Thessaloniki, Greece, GUARTE HELLAS SOLE SHAREHOLDER CO. P.C. (I.K.E.) operates at the strategic maritime crossroads of Europe, the Mediterranean, the Black Sea, and the Middle East.

We combine deep regional expertise in international sea transport with specialized wholesale trading in industrial wood products, structural construction materials, and architectural sanitary equipment—creating enduring value between producers and global markets.
